About Us

For over 30 years, Vedanshi Natu Kodi Eggs has been providing the finest naturally raised country eggs. Our hens are free-range, fed with organic grains, and raised in a stress-free environment.

We believe in quality over quantity, which is why every egg is hand-picked and quality checked before delivery.

  • 100% Naturally Raised
  • No Antibiotics or Hormones
  • Fresh Daily Delivery
  • Hand-picked Quality Check
